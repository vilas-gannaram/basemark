## experiments/

Proof-of-concept code. Rules:

- Nothing in `packages/`, `apps/`, or `examples/` may depend on anything here.
- No deprecation policy — anything in here can be deleted at any time, without notice.
- No stability guarantees, no versioning, no changelog entries.
- If an idea proves out, promote it into `packages/` deliberately (new PR, real package.json, tests).
